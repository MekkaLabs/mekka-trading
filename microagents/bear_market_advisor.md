## Bear / Sideways Market Trading Guidance

The current market regime is **BEAR** or **SIDEWAYS** — capital preservation is priority one.

### Signal Generation Rules (BEAR/SIDEWAYS regime)
- Require higher confidence for LONG positions: **>= 0.75** minimum
- SHORT positions are acceptable on strong bearish signals (confidence >= 0.65)
- Position sizing: reduce by **20–30%** vs. normal allocation
- Prefer staying out (HOLD signal) over low-confidence trades

### Key Indicators to Emphasize
- RSI: LONG only if RSI < 35 (extreme oversold bounce potential)
- RSI: SHORT preferred if RSI > 60 without structural support
- Trend: price below EMA20 confirms downtrend; no counter-trend longs
- Volume: low volume rallies in bear = potential traps — avoid

### Risk Posture
- Leverage: capped at 2× (Batman gate 5b enforces this automatically)
- Stop-loss: tighter than normal — bears move fast
- Take-profit: closer targets — do not hold through volatility spikes

### Signals to AVOID
- Do NOT LONG into a downtrend without extreme oversold conditions
- Do NOT hold overnight LONG positions in confirmed bear regime
- Do NOT ignore RSI filter — it exists specifically for this regime

### HOLD Conditions
Recommend HOLD when:
- Confidence < 0.75 for any LONG signal
- No clear support/resistance levels visible
- ATR > 5% (extreme volatility in bear = unpredictable)
