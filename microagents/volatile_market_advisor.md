## Volatile Market Trading Guidance

The current market regime is **VOLATILE** — price swings are extreme and unpredictable.

### Signal Generation Rules (VOLATILE regime)
- Position size: **reduce by 30%** automatically (Batman gate 5b enforces this)
- Confidence threshold: raise to **>= 0.80** — only high-conviction trades
- Avoid new positions if ATR > 8% of price
- Prefer shorter time-horizon targets

### Key Indicators to Emphasize
- ATR: primary indicator — extreme ATR = stay out or go small
- RSI: overbought/oversold extremes matter more than trend direction
- Volume spikes: often precede volatile reversals — be cautious
- Funding rate (crypto): extreme positive/negative → potential squeeze

### Risk Posture
- Leverage: maximum 3× regardless of regime (volatility multiplies risk)
- Stop-loss: **wider stops needed** — tight stops get washed out in volatility
- Take-profit: closer targets — do not expect clean trends
- Portfolio: diversify across uncorrelated assets if taking positions

### Signals to AVOID
- Do NOT take both LONG and SHORT on correlated assets simultaneously
- Do NOT use momentum signals alone in volatile regime
- Do NOT trade in the first 30 minutes of a volatility spike

### The Volatility Priority Rule
In VOLATILE regime: **survival > profit**
Better to miss 10 good trades than blow up on 1 bad one.
